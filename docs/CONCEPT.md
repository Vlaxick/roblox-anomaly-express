# Anomaly Express

Tycoon, survival, extraction, and collection game for Roblox.

## Core loop

1. Place a unique object or specimen on a free space on a personal base.
2. Wait for it to grow; its rarity determines its appearance and income.
3. Mature objects produce coins automatically.
4. Spend coins on more spaces and valuable objects.
5. In a later milestone, board the train every five minutes to recover new
   specimens from dangerous regions, return home and grow those finds.

## First playable milestone

- One personal autumn base per player, currently dressed for Halloween
- A clear place → grow → automatic coins → expand loop
- Three unique objects with visible rarity, growth time and income
- Two starting spaces, expandable to eight
- One catalogue for placement and expansion
- Player levels with movement-speed progression
- Train trips and recoverable specimens after the base loop is proven

## Current playable checkpoint

Six fenced bases are generated in a moonlit autumn clearing. Each player starts
with 40 coins and two spaces. A mimic chest costs 15 coins, grows for 10 seconds,
then pays three coins every five seconds. A rare wandering lantern and an epic
midnight clock take longer to grow and pay more. Rarity is explicit, with no random
purchase outcome. The objects have individual silhouettes and personalities.

The catalogue shows the actual object model, price, growth time and income together.
Players can place objects from the main button or select a space by approaching it.
Coins arrive automatically; there is no collection machine, energy currency,
reactor or storage management. Additional spaces are the current base progression.

The base keeps its rustic autumn layout, with dark stone pads, rusty foliage,
moonlight, candle lanterns, cobwebs and warm windows for Halloween. Pumpkins serve
as seasonal decoration. The interface uses dark plum surfaces, cream text and
burnt-orange actions. Saving, object replacement and train expeditions remain
later milestones.
