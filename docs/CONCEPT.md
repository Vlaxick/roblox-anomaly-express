# Anomaly Express

Tycoon, survival, extraction, and collection game for Roblox.

## Core loop

1. Grow anomalies on a personal laboratory plot.
2. Collect energy and improve the base.
3. Board the train that arrives every five minutes.
4. Explore a dangerous region and recover specimens.
5. Return before the train leaves.
6. Incubate specimens, discover mutations, and prepare for harder regions.

## First playable milestone

- One personal plot
- One Eye Flower anomaly
- One energy generator
- One train
- One expedition region
- One recoverable specimen
- Return and incubation flow
- Ten player levels with movement-speed progression

## First playable checkpoint

Six plots are generated when a server starts. Each player claims a free plot and
spawns beside its Eye Flower. The flower makes five energy every five seconds,
up to 100 stored energy. The owner collects it from the glowing collector and
earns five XP per collection. This is a temporary prototype loop; saving,
upgrades, train travel, and expeditions are the next milestones.
