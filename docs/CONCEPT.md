# Anomaly Express

Tycoon, survival, extraction, and collection game for Roblox.

## Core loop

1. Place a plant or specimen on a free bed on a personal farm.
2. Wait for it to grow; its rarity determines its appearance and income.
3. Mature plants produce coins automatically.
4. Spend coins on more beds and valuable plants.
5. In a later milestone, board the train every five minutes to recover new
   specimens from dangerous regions, return home and grow those finds.

## First playable milestone

- One personal autumn farm per player
- A clear plant → grow → automatic coins → expand loop
- Three plant types with visible rarity, growth time and income
- Two starting beds, expandable to eight
- One catalogue for planting and expansion
- Player levels with movement-speed progression
- Train trips and recoverable specimens after the farm loop is proven

## Current playable checkpoint

Six fenced farms are generated in a warm autumn meadow. Each player starts with
40 coins and two beds. A pumpkin costs 15 coins, grows for 10 seconds, then pays
three coins every five seconds. Rare mushrooms and epic golden apple trees take
longer to grow and pay more. Rarity is explicit, with no random purchase outcome.

The catalogue shows the actual plant model, price, growth time and income together.
Players can plant from the main button or select a particular bed by approaching
it. Coins arrive automatically; there is no collection machine, energy currency,
reactor or storage management. Additional beds are the current farm progression.

The base uses natural materials and warm daylight: wooden borders and fences,
tilled soil, red barns, orange pumpkins and yellow foliage. Interface panels use
paper tones, dark brown text and green action buttons. Saving, plant replacement
and the train expedition loop remain later milestones.
